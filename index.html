House rules

A few simple rules so every guest, and our neighbours, can enjoy the villa.

  1. Check-in 2:00 PM, check-out 11:00 AMEarly check-in or late check-out depends on availability. Please ask in advance.
  2. Maximum 7 guestsOnly registered guests may stay overnight. Day visitors need prior approval from the host.
  3. Valid photo ID for every guestAadhaar, passport, driving licence or other government ID. The primary guest must be 18 or older.
  4. No smoking indoorsSmoking is allowed only in open outdoor areas. Please use the ashtrays provided.
  5. Quiet hours 10:00 PM – 7:00 AMKeep music low and indoors after 10 PM. Loudspeakers outdoors are not permitted at night.
  6. Parties and events need approvalBirthdays and small celebrations are welcome. Tell us beforehand about decorations, extra guests or DJs.
  7. Pets allowedPets are allowed, but not in the swimming pool.
  8. Bonfire only with the caretakerThe caretaker lights and puts out the bonfire. Never leave a fire unattended.
  9. Look after the villaPlease do not move furniture or take towels and linen outdoors. Damage or missing items will be charged.
  10. Save powerSwitch off ACs, lights and the TV when you leave the villa.
  11. Lock upLock doors and sliding windows when going out. Keep valuables with you. The host is not responsible for lost items.
  12. Drink responsiblyIllegal substances are strictly prohibited on the property.

Pool rules

The pool is private to your group. There is no lifeguard, so please read these before your first swim.

  1. Pool hours 8:00 AM – 10:00 PMNo swimming after dark without the pool lights on.
  2. Swim at your own riskThere is no lifeguard on duty. Never swim alone.
  3. Children under 12 must be supervisedAn adult must be in or beside the pool at all times.
  4. No diving or jumpingThe pool is not deep enough for diving.
  5. No glass near the poolUse plastic cups only. Broken glass means the pool has to be drained, which may attract an extra penalty of ₹3,000 (INR 3,000).
  6. No swimming after drinkingAlcohol and swimming don't mix.
  7. Walk, don't runThe stone deck gets slippery when wet.
  8. Shower before you swimWear proper swimwear. No oils or heavy lotions in the water.
  9. No food in the poolSnacks stay on the deck or lawn.
  10. Leave the pool during stormsGet out at the first sign of thunder or lightning.
